## What the four videos are

All four are by one creator, **TheRealRaven**, and form one series of "matchup tree" guides. So this is one drafting
philosophy applied to four roles, not four independent opinions: agreement between the videos is consistency inside one
creator's framework, never independent corroboration.

Audience: solo-queue players choosing a champion for their own lane. Every statistic is ranked Master+ data the creator
reports (games, win rates, gold at 15); none is professional play, and no specific pro game is analysed, so nothing
belongs in Analyst Perspectives. Gameplay clips are ranked games used as illustrations.

The videos do **not** cover pick order, bans, blind versus counter pick timing, flex picks, team-wide composition
identities (beyond scaling and front-to-back hints), pro draft tendencies or itemization (beyond passing mentions such as
Runaan's Hurricane on Jinx). Those categories are left empty in Draft Insights.

## What each video teaches

### Top (vQCDKJx0rHM)
Five classes (Riot's labels): juggernauts, divers/bruisers, skirmishers/duelists, tanks, ranged top laners, each with a
purpose and a weakness. A tree (0:24–1:06): juggernauts beat tanks and lose to ranged; divers jump ranged but lose to
tanks; skirmishers beat tanks; tanks beat divers and ranged; ranged beat juggernauts. Then the main thesis: the class
win rates barely differ at Master+, and a single ability interaction decides more than the class (Garen E and Jax E,
Shen W and Malphite against auto-attackers, Gangplank W against a stun, Kennen E escaping a dive). Tanks lose lane and
still matter later, except against hyper scalers (Kayle over K'Sante). Ranged tops must convert a lead (Jayce against
Malphite). Practical advice: play one to three champions and learn their exact matchups.

### ADC (is1BFqUWweQ)
Five archetypes: scalers, lane bullies, poke/casters, skirmishers, mage bots. Grid (0:59): bullies beat skirmishers and
scalers, poke beats scalers and skirmishers but can die to bullies, scalers win nothing in lane. Mage bots dominate
lane on range and peak mid game; counterplay is that most are immobile. Draven's 3,500 gold lead that loses the Baron
fight frames the main lesson: a lead wins only when converted (bullies with 300+ gold at 15 win 63%). Scalers should
"lose lane as little as possible". Support choice: bullies want engage; pairing stats favour engage over enchanters,
which the creator himself explains as enchanters' lower base win rate (9:10), not proof that enchanters are bad.

### Jungle (jgHpbaX9wYE)
Five archetypes by goal, not by power curve: tempo gankers, power farmers, skirmishers, tanks, assassins. Every one
plays the same loop ("farm, gank when it's guaranteed") and differs in what it does with a lead. Tanks win from behind
(highest win rate when behind); assassins are feast or famine; tempo junglers must end before scaling comps take over
(Lee Sin up 2,000 gold who loses to Zac and enemy hyper carries at Elder). The jungle matchup itself is the least
important, because junglers can avoid each other.

### Support (D7DemQewZqM)
Six types: enchanters, engage, wardens, hooks, mage supports, misfits (Senna, Bard, Yuumi), plus off-role lane winners
(Pantheon, Camille, Zoe, Sylas, Elise). Each type has a plan and one tool. Tree (15:12): engage, wardens and hooks beat
enchanters; enchanters beat mages and misfits; anything that closes distance beats mages; wardens lose lane and win long
games. Named exceptions: Nautilus loses to other engage supports, Braum beats projectiles but loses to walk-in engage,
Janna beats engage but loses to hooks, Yuumi beats mages but loses to hooks and engage. Disengage is a cooldown, not a
counter (50% overall against engage). Pair the support with what the ADC wants. The fine print (level 2 on the ninth
minion, support quest, roaming, vision, control wards) is laning and itemization, not drafting, and is left out of
Draft Insights.

## Strongest insights (17 in the section)

1. The archetype gives the plan; the champion decides the matchup (support, top, ADC).
2. Popularity and win rate are not strength (Karma, Ezreal, Lee Sin, Jayce).
3. Play one to three champions per role and learn their exact matchups (top, support checklist).
4. Support types and their tree.
5. ADC archetypes and their lane grid.
6. Top lane classes and their tree.
7. Jungle archetypes: same loop, different goal; the jungle matchup matters least.
8. Ask whether your abilities work against theirs (Jax E, Shen W, Garen E).
9. Melee into ranged: can you close the gap? (Nasus W, Irelia, Pantheon; Kennen E, Gangplank W.)
10. Projectile blockers lose to walk-in engage (Braum, Nautilus).
11. Disengage is a cooldown, not a counter (Janna, Milio, Morgana, Poppy).
12. Immobile mages lose to anything that reaches them (support mages, mage bots, Pyke).
13. Off-role lane winners: refuse the level 2 fight.
14. Match the support to the ADC's goal (bully + engage, scaler + warden or enchanter; Ezreal + Nautilus 42%).
15. A lead wins nothing until it is converted (Draven, Jayce).
16. Scalers and tanks are judged by the late game, not the lane (Jinx, Kayle, wardens, tank junglers).
17. A tempo lead has a deadline (Lee Sin against Zac and hyper carries).

- Losing lane by design: scalers (ADC 3:50), tanks (top 7:38), wardens (support 5:21), tank junglers (jungle 4:48).

## Disagreements and tensions (kept, not resolved)

- **Enchanters with scalers.** Support 16:16: Wardens with a scaling ADC 57%, Lulu with Kog'Maw or Twitch very high,
  Janna great with Aphelios, but Aphelios with Lulu only 44%. ADC 3:17: hyper scalers have higher win rates with engage
  supports who also peel (Maokai, Braum, Thresh, Leona). ADC 9:10 then says the engage-over-enchanter pattern largely
  reflects enchanters' lower base win rate. Assumption difference: per-pair results versus type averages; the series
  never controls for the base rate, so neither reading is established.
- **Mage bot lanes.** ADC 8:06: mage bots (Karthus close to 60%, Vel'Koz, Aurelion Sol, Hwei, Syndra 54%+) dominate.
  Support 9:16: mage supports win lane and have the lowest type win rate (about 47%). Not a contradiction (different role,
  different gold), but the same "range wins lane" idea yields opposite game results by role.
- **Disengage counters engage?** The support video rejects the common claim (12:31: 50% overall) yet names Janna as a
  disengage that beats engage. The resolution it offers is usage (hold it for the engage), not type.
- **Assassins against skirmishers.** Jungle 4:15 says assassins lose to skirmishers early; 5:53 shows Kha'Zix dominating
  once ahead. Consistent once timing is attached; the insight keeps the timing.

## Fact status

- **Established game mechanics** (consistent with the champions' official ability descriptions; not re-checked against a
  patch note today): Braum's Unbreakable blocks projectiles, not dashes; Nautilus's Q stops on the first unit hit;
  Poppy's W stops dashes around her; Morgana's Black Shield blocks magic damage and crowd control, not physical damage;
  Milio's ultimate does not remove knock-ups; Jax's Counter Strike dodges basic attacks, not abilities; Shen's W blocks
  basic attacks; Gangplank's W cleanses crowd control.
- **Creator-reported statistics** (Master+ ranked, patch 26.21 or 26.22 as shown on screen, sample sizes mostly
  unstated): every win rate and gold figure in the section. Patch-dependent, not independently checked, and shown with
  the patch.
- **Creator opinions and classifications**: the archetype trees, which champion belongs to which type ("Riot's labels"
  for top only), "the jungle matchup matters least".
- **Strategic interpretations**: the "why it works" text in each insight, written for Draft Assistant and labelled as
  interpretation.
- **Unverified or outdated-risk claims**: support quest values and Bard chime timing (patch mechanics, left out); "Riot

  survive balance changes.
